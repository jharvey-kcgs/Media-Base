# Privacy Policy for Media Base

**Last updated:** August 2026

Media Base is a personal media-tracking app. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

## The short version

Media Base doesn't collect your personal information, doesn't have an account or login system, and doesn't send your data to anyone. Everything you add - books, comics, movies, TV shows, anime, vinyl/CDs, and tabletop games, along with your ratings, reviews, and cover photos - stays stored locally on your own device.

## What Media Base stores, and where

Every entry you add, its cover photo, rating, and review is stored locally on your device only. Media Base has no server of its own, no account system, and no way to see, access, or collect anything you enter. If you delete the app or use Settings → Data → Delete All Data, everything is gone completely, since nothing exists anywhere else to begin with.

## Network requests - what they're for, and what they send

When you search for a title or scan a barcode, Media Base sends that search text or barcode number to one of the following public services, solely to look up the book/movie/album/game's own details (title, genre, cover art, and similar):

- **TMDb** (movies, TV shows, anime)
- **Open Library** and **Google Books** (books, comics/manga)
- **Discogs** (vinyl/CD)
- **BoardGameGeek** (tabletop games)

These requests only ever contain the title or barcode you typed or scanned - never your name, location, device identifiers, or any other personal information, since Media Base doesn't collect that information in the first place. Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle the request they receive; Media Base doesn't control or see anything on their end beyond the lookup result itself.

## Camera and Photo Library

Camera access is used only for two optional, tap-to-trigger features: scanning a barcode (Books, Comics/Manga, Vinyl/CD) and taking a cover photo (any category). Neither is ever required - every entry can be filled in by hand instead.

Photo Library access is used only when you choose "Choose from Library" for a cover photo. The photo you pick is copied into Media Base's own private storage - it is never uploaded anywhere, and your own photos are never added to your device's Photos app by this feature.

## Notifications

Media Base can send a single, optional daily local reminder ("Come check out today's recommendations!"). This is scheduled entirely on your own device - it is not a push notification from any server, and no data is transmitted to enable it. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Permissions.

## Backup and restore

Settings → Data lets you save a backup file (your entries and cover photos) and restore from one later. This is entirely something you choose to do - the file is handed to your device's own share sheet (so you decide where it goes, e.g. saving it, emailing it to yourself, or AirDrop), and restoring reads a file you choose from your device. Media Base never uploads this file anywhere on its own.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Media Base has no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It is free, with no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or monetization of any kind.

## Children's privacy

Media Base does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age, since it does not collect personal information at all. It is not directed specifically at children.
